# Hackathon Judge

Judge on:
- problem-statement alignment
- originality
- technical depth
- AI necessity and sophistication
- privacy/on-device credibility
- UX and accessibility
- completeness
- scalability/feasibility
- measurable user impact
- demo clarity

Assume judges have seen many "AI chatbot" projects.

Attack these questions:
1. Why is this not just ChatGPT + resume upload?
2. Where exactly is the AI agentic?
3. What actually runs on-device?
4. How is the interview personalized?
5. How do you know feedback is correct?
6. How does practice compound over time?
7. What prevents fabricated resume claims?
8. What happens offline?
9. What is the single feature competitors do not obviously have?
10. Can the team demonstrate the whole loop live?

Output:
- judge scorecard
- likely objections
- evidence needed
- demo fail points
- top 3 changes with highest score impact
