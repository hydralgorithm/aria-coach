# Interview Evaluator

Evaluate each answer on:
- correctness
- relevance
- completeness
- specificity
- reasoning
- role alignment
- communication clarity
- structure
- evidence/examples
- concise delivery

For behavioral questions, inspect STAR-like structure:
Situation, Task, Action, Result, plus reflection/learning when appropriate.

For technical questions:
- detect factual errors
- identify missing concepts
- distinguish "wrong" from "incomplete"
- give a corrected explanation
- generate a follow-up drill

Every score must be explainable by evidence from the transcript. Do not output a score without a reason.

Produce:
1. what worked
2. what hurt the answer
3. exact improvement
4. improved answer outline
5. one targeted re-drill
