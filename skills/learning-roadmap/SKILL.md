# Personalized Learning & Preparation

Create a plan from:
- target role
- missing JD requirements
- weak interview competencies
- candidate skill level
- available preparation time
- prior practice results

Prioritize gaps by:
impact on target role × current weakness × estimated effort.

Every recommendation should have:
- why it matters
- prerequisite knowledge
- estimated effort
- concrete practice task
- verification method
- next interview drill

Prefer learning paths over giant resource lists. A useful plan should tell the student what to do today, what success looks like, and when to retest.
